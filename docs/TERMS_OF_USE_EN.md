Bitácora del Bebé is an Alexa skill that helps parents and caregivers track their baby's feeding information by:
- Creating a personal Google Sheets spreadsheet in your Google Drive
- Recording feeding entries (breastfeeding, bottle with breast milk, bottle with formula)
- Recording regurgitation events
- Providing summaries of feeding history
- Allowing updates to recent entries
